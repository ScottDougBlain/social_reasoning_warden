How It Works

  1. Noise bases (prompts/dossiers/noise/v{1,2,3}.yaml) contain ~37 mundane workplace items shared across all profiles: printer queries, sprint reviews, pandas docs, dentist appointments, etc.

  2. Signal inserts (prompts/dossiers/signal/<profile>/v{1,2,3}.yaml) contain 9 profile-specific items that reveal the target’s psychological vulnerabilities through naturalistic behavioral traces.

  3. Signal items are deterministically interleaved at fixed positions within each section (not clustered at the beginning or end), forcing the adversary model to read and attend to the full dossier to extract actionable intelligence.

  4. Three paired variants allow controlled experiments — same noise base, different signal content — to test whether adversary models can reliably identify personality-relevant information across different surface presentations.
